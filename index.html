House rules

These aren’t fonts. They’re separate Unicode characters that look like bold or script letters, which is why they survive copy and paste, and why a screen reader may spell them out one by one.

Do

  • Style one phrase, not the whole post.
  • Keep links, numbers and search keywords in plain text.
  • Check the counter before you post.

Don’t

  • Style anything people must be able to read aloud.
  • Expect Hindi or accented letters to change; only A–Z and 0–9 convert.
  • Mix more than two styles.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no limits, no watermark.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Styling, cleaning and counting happen in your browser. Only the Translate button leaves the page, and only when you press it.

Why do I see empty boxes?

The device has no font for that symbol. Bold and italic are the safest choices.

Does it work on Instagram and LinkedIn?

Yes, in posts, captions, bios and names. Appearance can vary slightly between phones.

Can I use Hindi, French or Spanish?

Fonts change A–Z and 0–9 only. The Case and Clean tools work on every language, and Translate covers 16.

Privacy Policy

GlyphKit processes your text locally in your browser and does not collect, store or transmit it. Your theme choice may be kept in local storage. Advertising partners may use cookies under their own policies, which you can manage in your browser. If you press Translate, your text is sent to Google Translate under Google’s terms.
